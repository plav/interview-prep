How the grading works

Enough experience
Mid-level, SE II or III, or a stated ask of 4 years or less.

Stretch
Senior title with no years stated. Worth applying; you may land at mid-senior.

Not enough yet
Lead, staff, principal, VP, or an ask of 5+ years.

Below your level
Graduate, placement, junior, or 1–3 years. Likely a pay cut.

Unclear
The listing gave no level at all.

Stack match
Strong: core tech you use now. Partial: some overlap (e.g. cloud, Python from projects). Gap: main language you haven’t used (Java, Ruby, React-heavy, C++, Rust). Unknown: stack not stated.

Top pick
Roles I rate as the best mix of fit, pay and job security for you.

Apply links
“Job page” opens the advert itself. Recruiter adverts (MCS, Ocho, ViVA) are applied for through the recruiter. “Board search” means I couldn’t find the advert itself.

Warnings
Closed, older or undated listing, SC clearance, heavy travel, AI-training gig work, early-stage startup, Ireland-based only, low pay. “Seen live 28 Oct 2026” means the advert was still up when re-checked that day; “Not found 28 Oct 2026” means it had dropped off the board it was found on, so it has probably expired.

Your status and notes
Set a status on each row (Interested, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected, Not for me) and keep notes in the details panel. “Not for me” rows stay hidden unless you pick that status chip or turn off the filter. Saved in this browser only, so export a CSV to keep a copy.

Fit score
Out of 8. Experience: enough 3, stretch 2, unclear or not enough yet 1, below your level 0. Stack: strong 3, partial 2, unknown 1, gap 0. Plus 1 for a job page link and 1 for a listed salary; minus 1 if flagged older, minus 3 if closed. A rough sort key, not a verdict.

Best fit
Enough or Stretch experience with a Strong stack match.

Work pattern
Read from the location text. Hybrid means set office days or a named office; Office or not stated means the listing only gave a city. Check the advert.

Pay in euros
Shown with a rough £ figure at €1 = £0.86 (mid-2026 rate). “Pay reaches at least” uses the top of the range. Day and hourly rates are not converted.

Closes / link
The last column shows the closing date when the advert gives one (almost none do; most boards simply drop adverts after about a month) and what the 28 Oct 2026 link check found. Rows marked not found are hidden by default; turn off “Hide not found” to see them.

Posted dates
Only some listings gave a date. It is shown under the role with its age at the 27 Oct snapshot. Sorting by date puts undated listings last.

Grading is my judgement from short listing snippets, not the full adverts. Recruiter listings (MCS Group, Ocho and others) often hide the client, and the same job can appear more than once under different titles. Stars, statuses and notes you add are saved in this browser only, so export a CSV before clearing browser data or changing device. Filters are kept in the page address, so a filtered view can be bookmarked.
